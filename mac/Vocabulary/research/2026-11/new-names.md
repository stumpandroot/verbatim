# November 2026: research notes

## What you'll find

`new-names.json` holds 14 candidates: 12 new terms and 2 that move existing forms (Grok 3 and Grok 4). All 14 went in. By confidence: 2 high, 11 medium, 1 low.

- **AI:** GPT-6, GPT-6.1, GPT-6.1 Terra, Gemini 4, Gemini 4 Argon, Claude Fable, and Claude Mythos.
- **Devices:** iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, Apple Watch Series 12, and Apple Watch Ultra 4.
- **General:** Rickroll.
- **Fix:** `grok 3` and `grok 4` were heard forms of Grok, so "grok 4" became "Grok" and dropped its number.

## Evidence

Every spelling is checked against a brand page title: openai.com, developers.openai.com, and help.openai.com for GPT; blog.google, deepmind.google, and ai.google.dev for Gemini; anthropic.com for Claude; apple.com newsroom for the devices; merriam-webster.com for Rickroll. openai.com, blog.google, anthropic.com, and merriam-webster.com refused direct fetches this month, so those spellings rest on the pages' own titles in search results plus release trackers (nocode.mba, Releasebot, Jetstream).

No source documents a speech engine's output for these names. They're in because each heard form is never ordinary English (it starts with "gpt", "iphone", "apple watch", or "claude", or it's a non-word), and the fix is casing or a hyphen.

## Left out on purpose

- **Already right:** AirPods 5, Siri AI, and Claude Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku 5.5 come out right with existing rules.
- **Ordinary words:** Apple HomeView ("home view"), Apple Home Collection, Gemini's Gems-to-Skills and Connectors renames, Meta's Muse Spark and Muse Wonder, and GPT-6 Sol, Luna, and Astra suffixes (sol, luna).
- **Couldn't verify on the brand's site:** iPhone Duo (MacRumors only), the PROLED XDR display name, and macOS 27's "Golden Gate" name (a section heading only; the form Apple uses in text is unclear).
- **M6 and A20 Pro:** "m6" is also a road, a rifle, and a bolt size.
- **Merriam-Webster's September 2026 words:** most are ordinary spellings (glow-up, vibe coding, cuffing season). Looksmaxxing is left out because the headword is "looksmax" and the variant spellings aren't confirmed. Copium and crashout have no misspelling evidence.
- **Specialty fields:** nothing this month. Medical, legal, and Bible and faith terms still wait for their switches in Settings.
- **Spelled-out "gemini four":** it can be astrology talk ("a Gemini, four Leos").
