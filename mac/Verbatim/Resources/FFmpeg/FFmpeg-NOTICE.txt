FFmpeg

Verbatim uses FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org) to read the audio in files that macOS and iOS can't open themselves, such as MKV, WebM, AVI, FLV, WMV, and Ogg. FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL) version 2.1 or later. The full license text follows this notice.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.

Version: FFmpeg 7.1.5, unmodified.
Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.5.tar.xz
SHA-256: de668509caf9e35e3cd162473441fdb29538c6d96ed080292b3cf9e6fc5d558f

Verbatim links FFmpeg dynamically. The libraries ship as separate frameworks inside the app (libavformat, libavcodec, libavutil, and libswresample), and you can replace them with your own build. On a Mac, sign the app again after replacing them (an ad hoc signature works: codesign --force --deep --sign - Verbatim.app), because macOS loads only libraries signed by the same developer as the app. The build script that produced them, with every option, is mac/ffmpeg/build.sh in Verbatim's source repository; Stump & Root Co. sends you a copy of it and of the FFmpeg source on request at hello@stumpandroot.co, for at least three years from the date you received this version of Verbatim.

Built with these configure options:
--disable-everything --disable-programs --disable-doc --disable-network --disable-autodetect --disable-debug --disable-asm --disable-avdevice --disable-avfilter --disable-swscale --enable-avformat --enable-avcodec --enable-swresample --enable-shared --disable-static --enable-pic --enable-small --enable-zlib --enable-protocol=file --enable-demuxer=matroska,mov,avi,flv,asf,ogg,mp3,wav,w64,aac,flac,mpegts,mpegps,amr --enable-decoder=aac,aac_latm,mp1,mp2,mp3,opus,vorbis,flac,ac3,eac3,alac,dca,wmav1,wmav2,wmapro,amrnb,amrwb,adpcm_ima_wav,adpcm_ms --enable-decoder=pcm_s16le,pcm_s16be,pcm_s24le,pcm_s24be,pcm_s32le,pcm_s32be,pcm_f32le,pcm_f32be,pcm_f64le,pcm_f64be,pcm_u8,pcm_s8,pcm_alaw,pcm_mulaw,pcm_s16le_planar,pcm_s24le_planar,pcm_s32le_planar,pcm_bluray,pcm_dvd --enable-parser=aac,aac_latm,mpegaudio,opus,vorbis,ac3,flac,dca,amr
(plus the target, compiler, and install-name options for each platform, which are in the build script).
