# Platform icon assets

These twelve SVGs are copies of **Font Awesome Free 6.7.2** brand icons, distributed as part of Font Awesome's free collection. Original project: https://fontawesome.com/ .

- Icon artwork: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0). See `FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt`.
- Trademark rights remain with the respective platform owners. Icons are used only to identify the external platform of a video; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
- Loom, Streamable, and Rumble are shown as clearly labeled initial badges because the bundled library did not provide authentic SVG marks.
- The colored background badges are Verbatim styling. The SVG brand geometries are not redrawn.

The app loads all icons locally and does not request them from external CDNs.
