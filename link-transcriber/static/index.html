ABOUT VERBATIM

How Verbatim works.

Paste a link to an accessible video. Verbatim gets the captions or transcribes the audio, keeps the original, and can add AI notes on request.

Captions first

Verbatim looks for existing captions before transcribing audio. Availability varies by platform and video, and captions can contain errors.

Originals stay original

Cleaning and summarizing never overwrite the source transcript. Exports say whether text is original or AI-edited.

Where your data goes

Transcription runs on the computer hosting Verbatim. Nothing is uploaded unless OpenAI is turned on for AI notes, and the app says so before you start. Saved items stay in this browser.

This is a responsive web app, not a native iOS binary. For iPhone access the backend must be reachable on a network. Safari’s Add to Home Screen can place a shortcut on your device.
