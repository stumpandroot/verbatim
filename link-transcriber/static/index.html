About Verbatim

Paste a link to an accessible video, or add a recording. Verbatim gets the captions or transcribes the audio, keeps the original, and can add AI notes on request.

Captions first

Verbatim looks for existing captions before transcribing audio. Availability varies by platform and video, and captions can contain errors.

Originals stay original

Cleaning and summarizing never overwrite the source transcript. Exports say whether text is original or AI-edited.

Where your data goes

Links and files you add are sent to the computer running Verbatim, transcribed there, and uploaded files are deleted when the job finishes. Nothing goes to a third party unless OpenAI is turned on for AI notes, and the app says so before you start. Saved transcripts stay in this browser only.

This is a responsive web app, not a native iOS app. For iPhone access the backend must be reachable on a network. Safari's Add to Home Screen can place a shortcut on your device.
